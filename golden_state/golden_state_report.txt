11. Theoretical non-reboot equivalents, not executed

These are research candidates only. They were not executed.

Candidate A: Per-slot CarrierConfig refresh/reload
  Target: CarrierConfigLoader for phoneId 1 / subId 11.
  Goal: reproduce CLEAR_CONFIG -> default fetch -> subscription updated.
  Risk: Medium. May affect active IMS capability calculations and can disturb WFC. If strictly subId-scoped it is less likely to affect the China SIM.
  Similarity to physical sequence: partial. It covers CarrierConfig, not UICC or IMS binder rebuild.

Candidate B: Trigger subscription refresh for slot 1
  Target: SubscriptionController / SubscriptionInfoUpdater.
  Goal: reproduce SUBSCRIPTION_CHANGED and active subscription map rebuild.
  Risk: Medium-high. Could change default data/SMS/voice selections or temporarily unset subId 11.
  Slot specificity: possible in theory, but OEM/QTI internals may fan out globally.
  Similarity: high for subscription portion.

Candidate C: Rebind/recreate IMS feature for slot 1
  Target: ImsResolver / ImsServiceController / MmTelFeature for slot 1.
  Goal: reproduce removeConnection/addConnection and MMTEL READY.
  Risk: High. May drop current WFC registration/calls and may affect global org.codeaurora.ims service if not truly slot-scoped.
  Slot specificity: theoretically slot/feature keyed; practical Binder APIs may not expose a safe per-slot public entry.
  Similarity: high for IMS portion.

Candidate D: Reset IWLAN network selection / AccessNetworksManager binding
  Target: AccessNetworksManager / vendor.qti.iwlan / telephony data network controller.
  Goal: force WLAN transport reevaluation for IMS APN.
  Risk: High. Can tear down IMS data network and affect data service routing.
  Slot specificity: likely subId-scoped at telephony request level, vendor service may be shared.
  Similarity: medium-high for IWLAN/ePDG portion.

Candidate E: SIM application / UICC refresh event simulation
  Target: UiccController / UiccProfile.
  Goal: force records reload as if SIM application refreshed.
  Risk: Very high. Could affect UICC app state and network attach. Not safe during golden-state capture.
  Slot specificity: can be slot-specific in architecture, but vendor side may touch modem-wide state.
  Similarity: closest to physical SIM removal/insertion if truly available.

Candidate F: com.android.phone process restart or vendor IMS daemon restart
  Target: Phone process / org.codeaurora.ims / imsdatadaemon / imsqmidaemon.
  Goal: recreate the telephony stack by process lifecycle.
  Risk: Very high. Not slot-specific; disrupts both SIMs and active WFC.
  Similarity: broad but too destructive.

Candidate G: Full reboot or radio service restart
  Target: whole device / radio stack.
  Risk: Highest; explicitly forbidden in this phase.
  Similarity: high but not a non-reboot mechanism.
