# X55 WFC StateSearcher History

These are exact Computer A historical experiment tools. They can change airplane/Wi-Fi state and are not approved for automatic execution.

- v3.0 through v3.4 are preserved under `legacy/`.
- v3.5 is the latest historical timing sweeper and is kept at this directory root.
- v3.4 produced 12 rounds, all classified P0.
- v3.5 produced a 16-round timing sweep, all classified P0.

The negative results are evidence about those specific experiments. They do not prove that WFC recovery is impossible, and v3.5 must not be described as a successful recovery tool.

Selected summary histories are preserved under `archive/computer_a_legacy/state_searcher_results/`. Large per-round logcat captures remain on Computer A and are hash-indexed in `docs/COMPUTER_A_RAW_EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.csv`.
